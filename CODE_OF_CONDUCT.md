# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help.

## What is expected

- Keep criticism on the code, not the person.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no. This is a small, opinionated project;
  "not a fit here" is a legitimate answer and not a judgement of you.
- Say what you measured. Disagreements about accuracy or latency are settled by
  running the fixtures, not by asserting harder.

## What is not

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning remarks, or unwelcome attention — in
issues, pull requests, commits, or anywhere else this project is discussed.

## Reporting

Email **burakmahyk@gmail.com**. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept
private. Responses range from a request to change behaviour to a permanent ban,
depending on what happened.

This is a one-person project; there is no committee and no appeals process.
That is a limitation worth knowing about in advance.
