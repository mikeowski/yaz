## Term repair leaves your Turkish alone

- **Turkish endings stay yours.** yaz no longer reads a Turkish ending as part of a term: "inputuna" stays "inputuna" (it was "Notion"), "mergele" and "deployla" stay as you said them.
- **A name keeps its ending.** When a term is heard with an ending and no apostrophe, the ending comes back after one: "Notiona" → `Notion'a`, "Körsırda" → `Cursor'da`, "Cloud koda sor" → `Claude Code'a sor`. Before, the ending was dropped.
- **Words that only sound like a term stay.** "kursörü" is the pointer, not Cursor; "Code'a sor" is not Cursor either.
- A name on screen no longer swallows the word after it: "Fitbit Air'ın" keeps its "Air".

The test set now has 26 sentences of developer Turkish and 5 names with endings; 0.2.0 got 10 of them wrong. `yaz eval --history` replays your own dictations through the new rules, on your Mac only.
