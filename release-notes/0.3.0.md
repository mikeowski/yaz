## Five main languages

- **English, German, Spanish and French** get what Turkish has: term repair guarded by the language's own word list ("doctor" stays doctor, "cours" stays cours), hesitation removal, numbers ("fifteen thousand dollars" → `15,000 dollars`, "quatre-vingt-dix" → `90`), voice commands ("new line", "neue Zeile", "nueva línea") and model instructions in that language. Pick it under Settings → Main language.
- A command after an article stays a word: "I need a new line." is a sentence.

## No dictation lost

- **Speak while the last one is still being written.** The new recording starts at once and waits its turn; dictations land in the order you said them.
- **A blocked paste stays on the clipboard.** With secure input on (a password field, a terminal's Secure Keyboard Entry) or Accessibility revoked, the text is kept on the clipboard and the menu says so.
- **Retry Last Recording** in the menu when a transcription fails, and a stuck transcription now gives up instead of holding every later one.
- **A dictation that waited lands only where you said it.** If you switched apps meanwhile, it stays on the clipboard.
- **A missed key-up no longer records the room** until the ten-minute limit.
- A dictation on the clipboard is kept out of clipboard managers and off your other devices.

## Fixed

- **Learning and the names on screen now work in Ghostty**, which answers Accessibility only through its own window.
- A typo in `config.json`, or an unreadable `learned.json`, is kept aside as `.broken-<date>` instead of being saved over.
- **Code mode in Grok Bot** and other Anysphere apps.
- `yaz mcp`: cancelling a question in the coding agent no longer takes your next dictation.
- "Neue Zeile, danke." is a line break again when Whisper joins the sentences with a comma.
